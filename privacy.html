Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you book a strategy call or contact us, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number and what you would like help with — along with basic technical information about your visit (including advertising click identifiers where you arrived from an ad).

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange and conduct your strategy call, provide our property research and buyers-agency services, and measure the performance of our advertising. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us operate — for example our scheduling, email and analytics providers — and with professionals you ask us to coordinate with, such as your broker, solicitor or accountant. Where required, we may disclose information to comply with the law.

Cookies and measurement

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the page and to measure advertising conversions. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at aaron@getaheadrealty.com.au or on 0466 191 055 and we will respond within a reasonable time.
